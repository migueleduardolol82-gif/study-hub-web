# Avatar equipment asset sources

The equipment geometry in `equipment-library.glb` is original work generated from
`assets/avatar/equipment-library.blend` and `scripts/build-avatar-equipment.py`.

Its packed PBR source materials use these CC0 assets from Poly Haven:

- `fabric_pattern_05`, by Rob Tuytel — https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_05
- `fabric_leather_01`, by Rob Tuytel — https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_leather_01

Poly Haven publishes these assets under CC0. Attribution is included here for
provenance even though the license does not require it.
